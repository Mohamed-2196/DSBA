5 · Icons

Construction

  • 24 px grid, 2 px safe margin (the dashed box). Stroke 1.75, round caps and joins, corner radii 1–3.
  • One tinted body (var(--icon-tint)) plus paper parts (var(--icon-paper)); lines and dots in currentColor. Arrows, chevrons and plain marks are line only.
  • Active: line navy (#14234D / #EAF0FF) at 1.85, body in the full art colour, paper takes the pill colour, on a pill, label bold in the section text tone. Three changes, never colour alone.
  • Sizes 24 (tab bar, top bar), 20 (sidebar, buttons), 16 (inline meta only).

6 · The desk-mates

Use them

  • Section headers (98–104 px): one mate, idle.
  • Empty states (160 px): sleepy, with a line that says what to do next.
  • Success toasts (40 px): happy, with a 6 px hop.
  • The Home shelf (56 px).

Don't

  • Two mates competing in one view, or a mate in Noora's chat.
  • A mate below 32 px: the nav icon takes over.
  • Failures and errors: a mate never smiles at a failed upload or a lost answer; use the plain error style.
  • New faces, people or mascots: Noora is the only person.
