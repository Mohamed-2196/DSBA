# DSBA "Launch Film" — shared brief (read fully before working)

## The situation (why this film exists)
Students of the DSBA program (Data Science & Business Analytics, BIBF, Bahrain) are running a
surprise for their tutors. World Teachers' Day is Monday 5 Oct 2026. On **Tuesday 6 Oct** all three
cohorts (~160 students) and all **17 tutors** are in one room for the first time ever.

Who believes what on Tuesday:
- **Tutors** were told the event is a surprise **birthday** for the program admin ("the Cool Admin").
- The **admin** was (supposedly) told the event is the launch of a student-built
  **newsletter + forum web app** she asked for. In reality she is in on everything.
- **Students** know the real target: the tutors. It's a Teacher's Day surprise.

A student rep gives a short speech, "launches" the app, and plays THIS FILM:
1. **Act 1 (0–64s)** — a slick, believable product-launch film for **DSBA Pulse**: the students'
   ground-up rebuild of their DSBA resource hub ("DSBA v2"), headlined by a newsletter + forum, plus an
   in-app file library, video lessons, calendar, grade calculator and search.
2. **Glitch #1 (64–68s)** — the launch "crashes"…
3. **Birthday (68–80.5s)** — …into a cute HAPPY BIRTHDAY card for the admin, with a music-box
   Happy Birthday. Tutors think: *this is the surprise we're in on.* People sing along.
4. **Glitch #2 (80.5–86.5s)** — the song is cut on the final "you". A terminal says the celebration
   target is wrong: expected *admin*, found *17 tutors*. Crescendo, flash, black, 1.5 s of silence.
5. **Act 3 (86.5–120s)** — a typed letter "Dear tutors… you were the surprise." → DROP →
   **HAPPY TEACHER'S DAY** → the 17 tutor names on the beat → thank-you outro.

Tutors get fooled twice. Students watch it happen. It must be genuinely impressive, funny, then moving.

## Hard specs
- 1920×1080, **30 fps**, **120.0 s** (3600 frames). H.264 MP4 + AAC.
- `cues.json` (generated by `tools/make_cues.py`) is the **single source of truth** for timing.
  Never hard-code a time that exists in cues.json; read it. If a time must change, change
  `make_cues.py`, re-run it, and tell the lead.
- `config.json` holds every name/placeholder (product name, admin, 17 tutors). Read from it.
  Tutor names are placeholders now ("Tutor Name 01"…); they will be swapped and re-rendered.
- **Secrecy**: the owner is fine with this project living on the app's feature branch later, but the film
  itself must never give the surprise away before the reveal (Act 1 must be a 100% believable launch film).

## Pipeline
```
src/ (HTML + GSAP, seekable)  --tools/capture.mjs-->  frames/*.png  (clean frames)
frames + cues.json  --tools/glitch_post.py-->  raster glitch on frames in g1/g2 ranges  --> ffmpeg
cues.json  --tools/audio/build_audio.py-->  out/audio/{music,sfx,mix}.wav
ffmpeg mux  -->  out/DSBA_Pulse_Launch.mp4
```
- The DOM film exposes `window.__film = { duration, fps, seek(t), ready: Promise }`.
  `seek(t)` must be deterministic (same t → same pixels). No CSS transitions/animations, no
  `Math.random()` without a seeded RNG, no real-time clocks. GSAP master timeline, paused.
- Raster glitch (RGB split, slice shifts, datamosh, pixel-sort, static…) is done in Python on top of
  the DOM frames, driven by `cues.g1` / `cues.g2` segments, stutters and hits. Designed elements
  (error windows, terminal text, scrambled letters) are drawn by the DOM scenes.
- Audio and video must agree on: chaos pops, typing ticks, countdown, stutters, tape-stop, glitch hits,
  the birthday melody (cut on the final "you" at 80.5), the 85.0–86.5 silence, the 95.0 drop, and the
  17 name chimes.

## Art direction (per act)
- **Act 1 — launch film**: cinematic dark navy (#05071a → #0b1033), brand navy #1a237e, electric
  indigo/cyan accents (#5c6bff, #4de1ff), crisp white type. Fonts: **Inter** (display/UI, tight
  tracking, 700–900 for punches), **JetBrains Mono** (labels, counters). Apple/Linear-style launch
  energy: depth, soft glows, 3D-tilted browser frames, light sweeps, motion blur-like easing
  (expo/power4), beat-synced cuts. Real UI screenshots of the new app float inside browser frames.
- **Glitch**: JetBrains Mono, retro OS error windows (generic, not Windows/Mac branded), terminal green
  (#39ff88) / alarm red (#ff3b5c), scanlines.
- **Birthday**: deliberately sweet & a bit cheesy — pastel (pink #ff9ecb, butter #ffe680, mint
  #9ff0d0, lilac #c9b6ff), confetti, balloons, an SVG cake with flickering candles, **Fredoka**.
- **Act 3 — Teacher's Day**: deep navy + warm gold (#f5c86b, #ffd98a), elegant **Playfair Display**
  (the site's own heading font) for the big moments, **Caveat** for the handwritten "Dear tutors,"
  letter or Playfair italic, gold particles / light rays, names on elegant cards. Emotional, grand.
